Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

StackSnap ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the StackSnap Chrome Extension.

1. Data Collection

StackSnap does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). All detection logic runs locally within your browser. We do not track your browsing history or send your data to any external servers.

2. Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • activeTab: To analyze the technology stack of the website you are currently viewing.
  • scripting: To execute the detection logic on the page.
  • storage: To save your scan history locally on your device (Pro feature).

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third-party services. The extension is entirely self-contained.

4. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, your data remains secure within your local browser environment.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@brilworks.com.
