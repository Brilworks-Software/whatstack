Common Questions

What makes WhatStack different from Wappalyzer?

WhatStack is optimized for the 2026 web ecosystem. It's lighter, uses Manifest V3, and places a heavy emphasis on zero-data collection. Our signatures are also updated more frequently for modern headless stacks.

Can I use WhatStack for competitive research?

Absolutely. It's designed specifically for developers and agencies to audit competitor sites, understand their infrastructure, and make informed architectural decisions.
